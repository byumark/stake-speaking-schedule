Stake Speaking Guidelines

Arrival: Please arrive 10–15 minutes early and check in on the stand with a member of the bishopric.

Speaking Order & Time: The Companion speaker addresses the congregation first (10–12 minutes), followed by the High Councilor (12–15 minutes). Please coordinate with the bishopric presiding officer.

Mingo Branch Note: Sacrament meeting begins at 10:15 AM at the Mingo Building. High Councilors speak individually (10–12 minutes) without an auxiliary companion speaker.

Gospel Doctrine: Please center your message on Jesus Christ and use the assigned monthly stake theme and referenced General Conference talks as doctrinal guides.

Substitutions: If you are unable to speak on your scheduled month, please coordinate with another leader in your organization or quorum to swap months.
